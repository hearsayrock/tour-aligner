# Project delivery phases

This document defines the release phases for Tour Aligner. It is the durable
product-level record of each phase's goal, scope, and definition of done.

## Phase 1 — Artist waitlist and profile preparation

### Goal

Build an engaged collective of artists before introducing the venue-facing
product. Tour Aligner's public presence in this phase is a branded artist
waitlist and a high-quality artist-profile experience that artists can prepare
and share.

Social content and other brand activity will keep waitlisted artists engaged
and build anticipation for the public launch.

### Audience and access

- **Artists:** May join the production waitlist, receive a confirmation email,
  create an account, and build and share their artist profile.
- **Public visitors:** May view artist profiles through their public,
  read-only links.
- **Administrators:** May access the full site for testing and operational
  purposes.
- **Non-admin account holders:** Are restricted to their profile experience;
  they must not be able to access the full product during this phase.

### Required capabilities

#### Waitlist

- A production-ready waitlist allows artists to submit their interest.
- Each successful waitlist submission receives an email confirmation.
- The waitlist process reliably records and manages submissions, including
  appropriate handling of invalid, duplicate, or failed submissions.

#### Artist accounts and profiles

- Waitlisted artists can create an account.
- After authentication, non-admin artists are directed only to their profile
  area.
- Artists can create a polished, personalized profile that functions as a
  small custom website for their work and identity.
- Artists can customize their page's imagery and colors.
- Artists can add a description of their art and express their aesthetic or
  vibe.
- Artists can add media samples, including songs and, where supported, video.
- Artists can publish lyrics.

#### Profile sharing

- An artist can share their profile with a stable link.
- Anyone opening a shared link has read-only access and does not need product
  access.
- The shared profile is suitable for posting to social channels or sending
  directly to collaborators, fans, and industry contacts.

### Definition of done

Phase 1 is complete when all of the following are true in production:

1. Artists can join the waitlist and receive confirmation emails.
2. Waitlisted artists can create accounts and are limited to their profile
   experience.
3. Artists can build rich, visually customized profiles that communicate their
   music, art, and identity through copy, images, colors, media samples, and
   lyrics.
4. Artists can publish and share those profiles publicly via read-only links.
5. Only administrators can access the full site for testing and operations.
6. The experience is polished enough to support artist acquisition and ongoing
   social-media engagement under the Tour Aligner brand.

### Out of scope for Phase 1

- Venue-facing product access and venue onboarding.
- General full-site access for non-admin users.
- Public rollout of the complete Tour Aligner marketplace or workflow.
